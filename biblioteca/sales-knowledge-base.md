## BOOK 1: "PSICOLOGÍA DE VENTAS" - BRIAN TRACY

### CORE PRINCIPLES
- **80/20 Rule**: 20% of salespeople make 80% of sales and earn 80% of the money. Top 20% earn 16x the average of bottom 80%.
- **80/20 of Sales**: 80% of sales success is psychological; only 20% is technique/product.
- **Self-Concept Theory**: There is a DIRECT relationship between self-concept and sales performance. You sell to the exact degree you consider yourself good at selling. Your income is proportional to how you perceive yourself.
- **"Nunca venderás más de lo que crees que vales"** - Core axiom.
- **Transfer of Enthusiasm**: Closing = transferring your enthusiasm into the prospect's mind. 51% of closing effectiveness = enthusiasm.
- **Sales Resistance is Normal**: At buying decision moment, fear/tension builds. Your job: get through it as quickly and painlessly as possible.
- **Rejection is NOT Personal**: "Some will; some won't; so what? Next!"

### 24 CLOSING TECHNIQUES

**PRE-CLOSES:**
1. **Closing on Appointments** - Telephone appointment-setting technique
2. **Approach Close** - Gets prospect to commit to a decision AFTER your presentation. Script: "Relax, I'm not trying to sell you anything right now. All I ask is that you look at what I have to show you with an open mind, and tell me at the end if this product makes sense."
3. **Demonstration Close** - Early conversation: "I could show you the best [product] on the market today. Are you in the position to invest [price] right now?"
4. **Hot Button Close** - 80% of buying decision determined by 20% of features. Identify the ONE key benefit and press it repeatedly.
5. **Trial Close** - Ask throughout presentation to gauge progress. "This copy machine makes 140 copies/minute. Would this be important to you?"
6. **Power of Suggestion Close** - Transfer excitement; enthusiasm is contagious.
7. **Invitational Close** - "Why don't you give it a try?" - Low pressure, trial mentality.
8. **Just Suppose Close** - "Just suppose price is not an obstacle... can we deal with that so I can finish my presentation?"
9. **Opinion Close** - Ask for their opinion on what you've shown.
10. **Showing Previous Customer Close** - Social proof from similar customers.
11. **Direct Close** - Simple, direct ask for the order.
12. **Summary Close** - "Let's briefly go over what we've talked about..." then summarize all benefits, then ask for order.
13. **Alternative Choice Close** - "Would you like the red one or the green one?"
14. **Standing Room Only Close** - Urgency: limited availability.
15. **Change Places Close** - "If you were in my position, with this great product, would you buy it?"
16. **Feel, Felt, Found** - "I understand how you feel. Others felt the same way. What they found was..."
17. **Negotiation Close** - Final terms to close.
18. **Take Away Close** - Remove something to create desire.
19. **Ben Franklin Close** - List pros vs cons. Most powerful because it mirrors how people actually make decisions.
20. **Order Blank Close** - Start filling out the order form while presenting.
21. **Assumptive Close** - Act as if they've already decided.
22. **Priority Stack Close** - Stack value before revealing price.
23. **Door Knob Close** - Used when you've almost lost the sale. Last-ditch effort to uncover the real objection.
24. **Zero Risk Close** - Remove all risk with guarantee.

## BOOK 2: "DOTCOM SECRETS" - RUSSELL BRUNSON

### THE SECRET FORMULA (4 Questions)
1. **WHO** is your dream client? (avatar, characteristics, pains, passions, desires)
2. **WHERE** can you find them? (congregations - where they hang out online)
3. **WHAT** bait will you use to attract them? (lead magnet, free offer)
4. **WHAT** result do you want to deliver? (transformation you provide)

### THE VALUE LADDER
Two axes: Price (X) and Value (Y). Move customers from bottom-left to top-right.

**Four Levels:**
1. **Bottom: Bait** - Free or very cheap ($0-$20). Lead magnet, free book. Gets people in the door. NOT designed to make you rich - designed to ATTRACT.
2. **Middle: Core Offers** - ($100-$5,000). Online courses, group coaching, membership sites, workshops. WHERE MOST PEOPLE BUY.
3. **Mid-High: Premium** - ($5,000-$25,000). Workshops, done-with-you programs.
4. **Top: Ultimate** - ($10,000-$100,000+). Mastermind, 1-on-1 coaching, done-for-you services. For people who want the highest level of support.

**Key Insight**: You're not teaching DIFFERENT things at each level - you're changing HOW people experience what you teach.

**Value Ladder Mission Statement**: "We help [who] achieve [result] through [method]."

**Example**: "We help fitness coaches build online businesses through sales funnels."

### 7 TYPES OF FUNNELS

**Frontend Funnels:**
1. **Two-Step Free-Plus-Shipping** - Sell at low end. Use Who/What/Why/How script. Great for cold traffic. 14% credit card pullout rate on step 2.
2. **Self-Liquidating Offer (SLO)** - $27-$97. Goal: frontend covers ad costs. Squeeze page → SLO page → Order form bump → OTO upsells.
3. **Continuity** - Recurring subscription. Best for free/$1 trial: Who/What/Why/How script. Without trial: Star/Story/Solution script. Upsells: yearly/lifetime accounts.

**Middle of Value Ladder:**
4. **Perfect Webinar** - 60-90 min. Can be recorded and automated. Content + sales pitch combined.
5. **Invisible Funnel Webinar** - Pay-after-webinar model.
6. **Product Launch** - Staged release creating anticipation.

**Backend Funnel:**
7. **High-Ticket Three-Step Application** - For $3K-$100K+ items.
   - Step 1: Video explaining results, why apply, how to apply
   - Step 2: Full application form (pre-sells the program)
   - Step 3: "Do your homework" page

### HOOK, STORY, OFFER FRAMEWORK
**Every page** in every funnel has these three elements. If something's broken, it's ALWAYS one of these three.

- **Hook**: Image/headline that stops the scroll. Grabs attention long enough to insert the story.
- **Story**: Builds connection, increases perceived value of what you'll offer. Start at PEAK EMOTIONAL STATE, then go back to backstory.
- **Offer**: The product/service as a solution. Make it irresistible.

**Application everywhere**: Ad, landing page, sales page, upsell, downsell, email - EVERY element.

### THE ATTRACTIVE CHARACTER (AC)
**4 Elements:**
1. **Backstory**: Where you came from. Relate to product.
2. **Speaks in Parables**: Life stories that teach and inspire (not just "what to do")
3. **Shares Character Flaws**: No one relates to perfection
4. **Power of Polarity**: Take sides on issues. Creates haters AND raving fans.

**4 Identity Types:**
1. **The Leader** - Guides audience A→B. Has achieved what audience wants.
2. **The Adventurer/Crusader** - Sets out to discover answers, brings treasures back.
3. **The Reporter/Evangelist** - Interviews experts, learns publicly ("The Oprah Formula")
4. **The Reluctant Hero** - Humble, doesn't want spotlight but shares anyway (Brunson's type)

**5 Storyline Types:**
1. Loss & Redemption
2. Us vs. Them (controversy)
3. Amazing Discovery
4. Secret Telling
5. Third-Person Testimonial

### SOAP OPERA SEQUENCE (5 Emails to New Subscribers)
Goal: Build bond between subscriber and AC quickly.

**Email #1 - Set the Stage**
- Thank you note
- Set expectations
- End with CLIFFHANGER

**Email #2 - Open with High Drama**
- Start at PEAK EMOTIONAL STATE (the crazy part)
- Rewind to backstory
- Hit a wall, found a solution
- Don't give the answer yet - OPEN THE LOOP

**Email #3 - Epiphany**
- Reveal the solution (leads back to core offer)
- Include link + CTA
- Add another cliffhanger

**Email #4 - Hidden Benefits**
- Benefits reader gets from knowing you/your product
- Use parables/stories
- Include link + CTA (or discount)

**Email #5 - Urgency & CTA**
- Create urgency: "Pulling the webinar offline", "10 seats left", "1000 books, most gone"
- Final push for immediate action
- Expire coupon in 24 hours

### SEINFELD EMAIL SEQUENCE (Daily emails after SOAP)
- Modeled after TV show Seinfeld - "emails about nothing"
- Daily emails about random life events
- EVERY email ties back to an offer
- Topics: embarrassing moments, purchases, travel, rages, funny past events
- Purpose: keep subscribers engaged daily while promoting products

### PERFECT WEBINAR SCRIPT
**Structure (3 Parts):**

**PART 1: INTRO (5 min)**
- Introduction of yourself
- Big Promise (the One Thing they signed up for)
- Hook to End: "At the end I'll give you a secret link for a transcript - only if you're watching live"
- Command Attention: "Sign out of social media, put phone in another room"
- Qualify Yourself: Why you're qualified
- Future Pace: Imagination exercises showing what life could be like

**PART 2: CONTENT - THE 3 SECRETS (45-50 min)**
- **The One Thing**: Core concept. Everything points back to this.
- **Secret 1**: Debunk false belief #1 → Share discovery → Teach STRATEGY (not tactics) → Show result (you or client)
- **Secret 2**: Debunk false belief #2 → Share strategy → Show result
- **Secret 3**: Debunk false belief #3 → Share strategy → Show result

**Key**: You do NOT teach tactics - tactics are what they BUY at the end.

**PART 3: CLOSE - "LET ME ASK YOU A QUESTION"**
- Humor break: "How many of you want this?" (show fire hose image)
- Ask PERMISSION to share: "Can I share something with you?"
- **THE STACK**: Keep stacking offer value
- **IF/ALL**: "If ALL [product] did was [one benefit], would it be worth $[stack total]?"
- **PRICE REVEAL**: "I'm not going to charge you $1,997, I'm only going to charge you $997"

### MAGIC BULLET WEBINAR REGISTRATION SCRIPT
